# Why Do Holo Cards Warp? Film-to-Paper Strain Difference, Gentle Recovery and Storage Parameters

Published 2026-11-14

![Cover](<https://www.yaykey.com/_astro/cover-geometry-2.DZsgLH2b.webp>)

Holo card warping is often blamed on moisture or on "bending toward the holo film side," but it actually comes from the strain difference created when the film layer and paper backing absorb and lose moisture. This article breaks down how to read warp direction, the 45%–55% RH conditioning range, and gentle recovery actions.

# How to Store Trading Cards Long Term: Sleeving, Humidity Control and a First-Recheck Timeline

Published 2026-11-15

![Cover](<https://www.yaykey.com/_astro/cover-collect-3.3NIQ65wF.webp>)

Complete long-term trading card storage on a timeline: prepare tools and environment, follow four sleeving and boxing steps, then run a 48–72 hour first recheck and acceptance comparison, using hygrometer readings and card-face observation to decide whether adjustment is needed. The goal is to place cards into a stable, recheckable storage state: after sleeving and boxing are complete, perform a first recheck within 48–72 hours, then review quarterly.

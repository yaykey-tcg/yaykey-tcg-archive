# Trading Card Humidity Control: Symptom Grading and Handling of Moisture Warping

Published 2026-11-15

![Cover](<https://www.yaykey.com/_astro/cover-geometry-3.4GRmuLBw.webp>)

Starting from symptoms such as card face warping, edge whitening and layer separation, handle trading cards by humidity risk grade, with 45%–55% RH humidity adjustment, a first recheck at 48–72 hours, and long-term storage conditions of 18–22°C. If you open a card box and find a stack of cards bowed as a whole, don't rush to press them flat.
